# TIDE — ISLAND SPRINT / Low-poly art direction

## References consulted
- Kenney Nature Kit: https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit — crisp silhouettes, deliberately limited colours, faceted geometry.
- Quaternius Ultimate Animated Character Pack: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatedanimatedcharacter.html — exaggerated readable heads, simple faces, distinct hair/costume silhouettes. Reference image inspected, not imported.
- Quaternius stylized nature: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatestylizednature.html — cohesive environment kit approach.

These are visual references only. No third-party models or textures are included. All GLB geometry and animation are generated by our Blender script.

## Original direction
A sunny island regatta: turquoise water, cream board decks, coral race markers, whitewashed blue-shuttered houses, a small lighthouse, faceted sand and palms. Flat-shaded polygons, texture-free vertex colours, no neon HUD or photorealistic surfaces.

Three equal-performance selectable athletes: LEO (cap, coral vest), MAYA (ponytail, golden vest), NOA (curls, teal vest). All have baked Idle, Paddle_Left, Paddle_Right and Celebrate clips. Joint-node animation rather than skeletal skinning; editable Blender controls are preserved.

Blender is the source of all characters, boards and placed coastal props. BabylonJS supplies the sea shader, wake/splash effects, cameras, lighting and race integration. The game is an original implementation, not a copy of a reference game.
